Thursday 5 November 2026 · Girona

BREVET NIGHTS 5

ORGANISED BY Brevet Rides Cup

Every Thursday night, October & November 2026. Some rides aren't meant to finish before sunset.

Every Thursday evening we meet at Velodrom Odeon at 18:45 and roll out at 19:00 for two hours of riding through Girona after dark — exploring quiet roads and routes that feel completely different once the daylight disappears. The same night, Velodrom Barcelona rides too.

A weekly night ride inspired by Rapha and powered by Exposure Lights. Brevet is made for the long days: endurance riding, ultra-distance adventures and the moments when the kilometres continue long after sunset. Exposure Lights keeps the adventure going when the light disappears.

We finish each ride at Hors Catégorie at 21:00, where bikes stop and the evening continues over food, drinks and stories from the road. No racing. No rushing home before dark.

SCHEDULE

  1. 18:45 — Meet: Velodrom Odeon
  2. 19:00 — Start
  3. 21:00 — Finish: Hors Catégorie
