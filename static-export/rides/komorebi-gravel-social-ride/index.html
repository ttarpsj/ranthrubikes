HERE WE GO AGAIN.

To welcome the autumn-winter season, we're bringing back a special edition of our classic KOMOREBI. One for our friends, our community, and everyone who knows that the best gravel rides always end with a beer, good food, and a few stories worth telling.

Back to our roots. Gravel, good friends, plenty of laughs, and absolutely no intention of behaving ourselves. Well… there might be a few regrets.
