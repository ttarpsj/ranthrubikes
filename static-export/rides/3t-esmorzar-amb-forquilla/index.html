3T RIDE: ESMORZAR AMB FORQUILLA TAVERNA DEL SUBIRÀ

ORGANISED BY Velodrom Girona CC

There's no better way to welcome 3T to Catalunya than the way we locals do it: incredible gravel, soulful food, and a day that feels more like a celebration than a ride.

We’ll roll out early from Velodrom Odeon in Girona and leave the familiar roads behind, heading deep into Les Guilleries. Forest trails, hidden backroads, long climbs and the kind of gravel that only locals know.

Our destination: Taverna del Subirà. Hidden in the forest, this old-school Catalan tavern is the perfect place for a proper esmorzar de forquilla — local embotits, grilled dishes, food from the land and maybe a glass of red if the moment calls for it.

We ride there to sit down, eat properly, share stories and enjoy Catalunya the way it should be enjoyed. Then we get back on the bikes. This is more than a ride. This is how we do gravel in Catalunya.
