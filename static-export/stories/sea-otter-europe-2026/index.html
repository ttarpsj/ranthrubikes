Sea Otter Europe 2026: A Celebration of Cycling Community

Each September, Girona becomes a gathering place for riders, brands, teams and cycling enthusiasts from across Europe. Sea Otter Europe 2026 continued that tradition, bringing together competitive racing, new equipment, community experiences and the culture surrounding the sport.

Across the weekend, visitors explored new collections, met professional teams and took part in activities that extended well beyond the exhibition stands. Movistar Team, Klimatiza Orbea Team, 226ERS and Canyon were among the names contributing to the atmosphere.

Beyond the Exhibition

One of the defining characteristics of Sea Otter Europe is the opportunity for direct interaction between the cycling industry and the people who ride its products. Professional athletes, recreational cyclists and curious newcomers share the same space, creating conversations that rarely happen in a conventional retail environment.

Throughout the event, visitors could attend a Q&A with the Movistar Gravel Squad, participate in a track-stand balance challenge alongside Klimatiza Orbea Team, and explore creative activities including live tote-bag customization and temporary tattoos.

These smaller experiences helped make the event feel less like a conventional trade show and more like a gathering of people connected through cycling.

Racing and Celebration

Competition remained an important part of the weekend. David Valero delivered one of the event’s headline performances with victory in the Sea Otter Europe UCI Marathon World Cup race, adding to the excitement of an edition reported to have attracted close to 80,000 visitors.

The weekend also offered an opportunity to celebrate Movistar Team’s recent success at La Vuelta a España. A gathering organized with 226ERS and Canyon brought together riders and supporters for pizza, drinks and a live DJ set.

The Value of Coming Together

What makes Sea Otter Europe significant is not simply the number of exhibitors or the scale of the racing. It is the concentration of different cycling communities in one place.

From professional teams and equipment manufacturers to local clubs and everyday riders, the event offers a reminder that cycling is shaped as much by shared experiences as by competition.

For Girona, another edition reinforced its position as a meeting point for European cycling culture.
